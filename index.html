FAQ

Got Questions?

What is a Digital Human ID?▾
A Digital Human ID is a verified certificate proving you are a real human — not an AI or bot. Use it for KYC verification on financial platforms, DAOs, and any service restricting access to real humans. It is completely free forever.
Is my data safe when I verify for Human ID?▾
Yes. Any documents or personal information submitted during Human ID verification are permanently and automatically deleted from our servers the moment your KYC is approved. We never store, sell or share your identity documents.
How do the 5 free scans work?▾
Every new account gets 5 free scans per tool — so 5 text scans, 5 image scans, 5 video scans etc. Human ID is always free and does not count against your quota. Once free scans are used, upgrade to Pro ($4.99/month) or Lifetime ($9.99 once).
How do the points and rewards work?▾
Every scan earns 2 points. Points unlock tier badges (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond) and can be redeemed in the Rewards Shop for free Pro trials, lifetime access, and exclusive badges. Points never expire.
Does it work on mobile?▾
Yes — DetectAI is fully responsive and works on all modern mobile browsers. No app download needed.
