FAQ

Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about DetectAI, Human ID, and how our platform works.

What is a Digital Human ID and why do I need one?▾
A Digital Human ID is a verified certificate that proves you are a real human being — not an AI agent or automated bot. In an era where AI can mimic human writing, speech and appearance, your Human ID gives you a way to prove your identity for KYC (Know Your Customer) purposes on financial platforms, DAOs, online communities, and any service that restricts access to real humans. It's completely free forever.
Is my personal data safe when I verify for Human ID?▾
Absolutely. Any documents or personal information you submit during the Human ID verification process are permanently and automatically deleted from our servers the moment your KYC is approved. We do not store, retain, sell, or share your identity documents under any circumstances. Your privacy is our top priority.
How accurate is the AI detection?▾
Our detection tools are powered by Claude AI, one of the most advanced language and vision models available. For text, we achieve approximately 92–96% accuracy on GPT-4 and Claude-generated content. For images, we detect most major AI image generators with high confidence. Video and voice detection accuracy varies based on the quality of input signals provided — adding a description significantly improves results.
What counts as a "scan" and how do the free trials work?▾
Each time you run an analysis on any of our detection tools — Text, Image, Voice, Video, Instagram, or TikTok — that counts as one scan. Every new user gets 5 free scans per tool (e.g. 5 text scans, 5 image scans, etc.). Human ID verification is always free and does not count against your scan quota. Once your free scans are used, upgrade to Pro ($4.99/month) or Lifetime ($9.99 once) to continue.
How does the points and rewards system work?▾
Every time you use any of our detection tools, you automatically earn 2 points. Points accumulate and unlock tier badges (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond). You can redeem points in the Rewards Shop for real perks including free Pro trials, lifetime access, and exclusive founding member badges. Points are tracked per account and never expire.
Can I use DetectAI on mobile?▾
Yes — DetectAI is fully responsive and works on all modern mobile browsers. You can access all detection tools, run scans, and manage your Human ID directly from your smartphone or tablet without needing to install any app.
How is my Human ID different from a regular digital certificate?▾
Unlike a standard digital certificate, your Human ID is backed by an AI-powered challenge that tests for lived human experience — something no AI can currently fake authentically. It includes a Humanity Score, a cryptographic SHA-256 hash for tamper-proof verification, a unique ID number, and an expiry date. It's designed to be a globally portable proof of humanity, not just a login badge.
